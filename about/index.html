About Soukai LLC

Focused software for practical decisions.

Soukai LLC is a software and product company focused on practical digital tools.

Our approach

We value clarity, restrained technology use, privacy-conscious design, and useful outcomes. We build focused products around concrete everyday problems, with communication that is direct about their purpose and limits.

Today

Our current product is Is This a Scam?, a mobile app designed to help users evaluate suspicious messages, links, and screenshots.

Looking ahead

Future Soukai products may expand into other practical decision-support tools. We will describe them publicly when they are ready.
