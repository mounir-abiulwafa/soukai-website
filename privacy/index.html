Website privacy

Privacy at getsoukai.com

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Scope

This policy applies to the Soukai LLC corporate website at getsoukai.com. Product-specific privacy practices are governed by each product’s own privacy policy.

Information this website intentionally collects

This website does not provide accounts, a contact form, advertising, or cookies intentionally set by Soukai LLC. It does not ask visitors to submit personal information through the website.

Limited website analytics

Soukai uses limited first-party website analytics to understand page visits and marketing-source effectiveness. The website may send a page path, referring hostname, allowlisted UTM campaign parameters, and a store-click destination when store links become available. Events are sent through Soukai's backend and processed using PostHog.

These analytics events do not intentionally send full referrer URLs, page query strings, IP addresses to PostHog, user-agent strings, or persistent user identifiers. The tracking implementation does not use cookies, localStorage, or sessionStorage.

Email

If you email Soukai at support@getsoukai.com, you voluntarily provide information through your email message. We use that information to respond to your inquiry.

Hosting and technical data

Hosting and infrastructure providers may process ordinary technical request data needed to deliver and secure this website, such as IP address, browser information, and request logs. Soukai LLC does not claim that infrastructure providers collect no technical data.

Product privacy

For Is This a Scam?, please read the Is This a Scam? Privacy Policy.

Contact

For website privacy questions, contact support@getsoukai.com.
