Is This a Scam?

A second opinion before you click, reply, or pay.

Is This a Scam? is a mobile app that helps users evaluate suspicious messages, links, and screenshots and receive an explainable risk assessment and practical next steps.

What it can help assess

  • A pasted suspicious message
  • A pasted link
  • A user-selected screenshot

What it returns

  • A LOW, SUSPICIOUS, or HIGH risk assessment
  • Clear reasons for the assessment
  • Recommended next steps

Languages

Is This a Scam? supports English, Arabic, French, Spanish, and Italian.

Important limitations

AI-assisted assessments can make mistakes. They should not be treated as a guarantee that content is safe or fraudulent.

Privacy and user choice

Users explicitly choose what to analyze. The app does not automatically scan messages, email, or the device. Submitted content is processed for the requested analysis after consent.
