Use it as a second opinion

AI-assisted assessments can make mistakes. They are not a guarantee that content is safe or fraudulent, and they do not automatically block scams. Use the reasons and recommended action to help decide what to do next.

Privacy and user choice

You choose what to analyze. The app does not automatically scan messages, email, or your device. Submitted content is processed for the analysis you request after your explicit action.
